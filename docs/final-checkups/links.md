## Findings And Tasks

**LI-01 - Confirmed responsive and text-reflow clipping, P2, locked page.** At 320px the Links content grid's auto min-content track grows beyond its available 268px, drawing the navigation/card right edge past the viewport (first card x=43..332). At 390px and 200% root text, cards can extend to x=606. The long, unbroken email and fixed icon/action widths contribute to intrinsic sizing; merely hiding horizontal overflow or changing the outer track cannot solve the clipped inner content. **Task:** seek explicit owner unlock for a scoped Links-only containment fix. Allow the profile/nav grid track and link text to shrink or break as needed while preserving the approved 375px+ appearance, full email label/accessibility and 92px card/touch targets. Retest 320/375/390 in light/dark, 200%/400% zoom, keyboard/touch, screenshot geometry, CTA and all existing locked Links tests. Roll back the scoped style if the approved desktop/card layout changes. **Status: CONFIRMED, NOT CHANGED (locked).**
