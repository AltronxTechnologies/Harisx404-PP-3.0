## Executive Overview

**Mail-Lens AI** (formerly PhishGuard AI) is an advanced Natural Language Processing and Explainable AI (XAI) email threat intelligence system engineered by **Muhammad Haris**. Developed as the final capstone project for the **KPITB AI Fellowship** (Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa), it classifies incoming emails into **Legitimate**, **Phishing**, and **Malicious** threat tiers while providing transparent, human-readable explanations of its decisions.

## Machine Learning Benchmark & Validation

The production model was selected through a rigorous 5-experiment empirical benchmark evaluated on identical stratified partitions:

| Experiment | Model Architecture | Feature Representation | Accuracy | Macro F1 | Status |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| E1 | Logistic Regression | TF-IDF (10,000 n-grams) | 97.33% | 0.9717 | Baseline |
| E2 | Multinomial Naive Bayes | TF-IDF (10,000 n-grams) | 96.05% | 0.8922 | Baseline |
| **E3** | **Calibrated Linear SVM** | **TF-IDF (10,000 n-grams)** | **97.94%** | **0.9760** | **Production Champion** |

## Explainable AI (XAI) in Action

Rather than presenting an uninterpretable score, Mail-Lens AI outputs:
1. **Calibrated Confidence**: Mathematically normalized probability distribution across the three classes.
2. **Visual Threat Chips**: Top positively-attributed tokens (e.g., `verify account`, `urgent action`, `suspended`) highlighted in crimson, and benign tokens in emerald.
3. **Analyst Briefing**: 4 automated bullet points outlining the specific triggers (e.g., *"Detected brand impersonation targeting Financial Services with an urgent 24-hour deadline"*).
4. **Actionable Remediation**: Concrete containment recommendation (e.g., block sender domain, quarantine mailbox, or safe to deliver).
