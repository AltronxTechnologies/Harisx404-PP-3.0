## Executive Overview

**MedicaLink HMS** is an enterprise-grade, multi-tenant B2B SaaS Hospital Management System architected and developed by **Muhammad Haris**. Designed for clinical networks, private hospitals, and healthcare systems, it manages the full continuum of care from outpatient scheduling to ICU triage, lab telemetry, and billing.

The platform provides complete tenant isolation, HIPAA-ready architectural patterns, real-time collaboration, and clinical AI acceleration within a modern monorepo.

## Comprehensive Module Ecosystem

MedicaLink HMS provides 25+ integrated clinical and administrative modules:

1. **Multi-Tenant Administration**: Tenant provisioning, custom branding, subscription tiers, and audit logs.
2. **Clinical Workflows**: Electronic Health Records (EHR), SOAP consultations, ICD-10 diagnostic coding, and digital prescriptions.
3. **Emergency & ICU**: Manchester triage classification, real-time ambulance dispatch tracking, and critical ventilator metrics.
4. **Telemedicine**: Low-latency peer-to-peer WebRTC video consultations with encrypted chat and waiting rooms.
5. **Smart Pharmacy**: First-Expired, First-Out (FEFO) dispensing, batch tracking, narcotics registry, and automated reorder alerts.
6. **Laboratory Diagnostics**: Sample barcoding, automated delta checks, and pathologist digital sign-off.
7. **Billing & Invoicing**: Multi-currency invoicing, insurance claim processing, and automated ledger balancing.

## Security & Defense-in-Depth

- **Tenant isolation:** Dynamic database connection resolution is designed to separate tenant data. Verify the isolation tests before describing it as a guarantee against cross-tenant access.
- **Authentication:** In-memory JWT access tokens, rotating HttpOnly refresh cookies and optional TOTP 2FA.
- **Input sanitization:** `express-mongo-sanitize` on API requests and DOMPurify for rendered content.
- **Sensitive data:** Patient identifiers and insurance numbers are described as encrypted with AES-256-GCM; verify the implementation and key management before publishing this claim.
- **Audit trails:** Clinical reads and writes are described as logged with user ID, role, IP and timestamp; verify retention and tamper protections before calling the logs immutable.
